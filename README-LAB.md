# paint-lab — provenance & purpose

This repository is a **lab copy of [1j01/jspaint](https://github.com/1j01/jspaint)**
(MIT license, retained in `LICENSE.txt`), snapshotted with its full history.
It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or contributing back to upstream** —
it exists so that a synthetic AI engineering team (the wawalu agent lab) can
iterate on a genuinely *pre-existing* product codebase while wawalu.org
measures the relationship between AI spend and shipped product change.

The research question, paraphrasing Uber's Andrew Macdonald: AI makes
engineers faster, but does it ship product faster? Every commit after the
snapshot point is agent-era; everything before it is the human baseline.

Do not open issues or PRs here expecting upstream jsPaint maintenance.
Lab scaffolding lives in `.lab/`, `.github/`, and `.agent-policy.json`;
everything else is the product.
